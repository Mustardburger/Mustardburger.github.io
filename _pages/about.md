I'm a senior undergraduate student at the University of Cincinnati majoring in Biomedical Engineering and minoring in Computer Science looking for PhD openings in fall 2025. 
\
\
My research interests are focused on two main branches (please visit Projects and and Publications pages):
* **Computational methods for functional genomics, gene regulation, and multi-omics data analysis**: I am particularly passionate about gene regulatory mechanisms and using computational tools to study the gene. My past experiences involve building deep learning models for cell-type specific transcription factor binding site predictions, differential equation modeling for measuring drug perturbations in cancer, and integration of massive single-cell gene expression datasets in Alzheimer's Disease.

* **Medical imaging**: I have a broad interest in biomedical image analysis, utilizing deep learning algorithms for disease diagnosis and automation. In previous projects I worked on brain tumor segmentations with multi-modal MRI scans, generative methods for liver ultrasound images, 3D reconstruction of human organs from freehand ultrasound images, and whole slide imaging stitching from prostatectomy.

Kowalewski and Haney** on the medical imaging side. I would also like to thank **Dr. Lee** for his lectures from BME 5010 on MRI physics.
